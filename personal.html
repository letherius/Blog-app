FOCUS

Stop Waiting for Motivation

5 MIN READ PRODUCTIVITY

Motivation is useful. It is also unreliable.

A better focus system reduces the number of decisions required to begin. Choose one task, remove the most obvious distractions, set a reasonable block of time, and start before giving yourself time to negotiate with the plan.

Starting becomes easier when the decision to start was already made.

Techniques like Pomodoro work because they give concentration a visible finish line. The exact number of minutes matters less than creating a short period where only one task is allowed to compete for attention.

SYSTEMS

Goals Point. Systems Move.

6 MIN READ CONSISTENCY

Goals provide direction. Systems decide whether you keep moving.

Wanting to learn something does not create study time. Wanting to get stronger does not create a training schedule. Wanting to build something does not decide what work gets finished tonight.

A useful system turns an intention into an action that can be repeated. Once the routine exists, progress no longer depends on deciding from scratch every day.

ENVIRONMENT

Your Workspace Is Part of Your Workflow

4 MIN READ ENVIRONMENT

Focus is not only something happening inside your head.

Your environment can make useful work easier or harder before discipline is ever required. Frequently used tools should be easy to reach. Distracting tools should require more effort to access.

Reduce the number of decisions between sitting down and doing the work.
